Travel Packing Checklist

Check these the night before you leave and the airport goes smoothly.

Got everything?

  • Passport or ID, plus screenshots of your flight and hotel bookings
  • Phone charger, power bank (carry-on only) and a plug adapter
  • Everyday meds: stomach pills, painkillers, bandages
  • Toiletries in small bottles (100 ml or less for carry-on)
  • One day of clothes in your carry-on
  • A card that works abroad and a little cash
  • A folding tote or zip bags for souvenirs and wet clothes

Once you're packed, lift the bag once. If it's heavy, take out one piece of clothing first.
