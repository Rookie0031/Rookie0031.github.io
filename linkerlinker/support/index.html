Common questions

Where are my saved links stored?

LinkerLinker stores your saved links, folders, categories, and notes in your private iCloud database when iCloud is available.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the unlock screen in LinkerLinker and select Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I delete a saved link?

Swipe left on a link in its folder and select Delete. Deleting a folder can also remove the links and subfolders inside it.
