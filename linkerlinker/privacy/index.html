Privacy policy

Your saved links stay yours.

Effective September 6, 2026

Information LinkerLinker stores

LinkerLinker stores the links, titles, notes, folders, categories, and preview-image addresses that you create or save in the app. This information is used only to provide the app's saving, organizing, searching, and syncing features.

iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, LinkerLinker uses Apple's CloudKit service to sync your saved content through the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account. The app does not create a separate LinkerLinker account.

Link previews

When you choose to fetch a preview, the app requests the web address you provide to retrieve publicly available page details such as a title or preview image. Those requests are made directly from your device to the relevant website or YouTube. We do not operate a server that receives your saved links for this feature.

Purchases

LinkerLinker offers an optional one-time purchase through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

What we do not do

LinkerLinker does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. It does not sell your personal information.

Deleting your information

You can delete links, folders, categories, and notes from within the app. To remove synced information from iCloud, delete the relevant items in the app while signed in to iCloud.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact The Quokka Company at contact@thequokkacompany.com.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The current version will always be available on this page.
