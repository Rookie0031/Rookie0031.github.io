Privacy policy

PointerBeam Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 (previous version: October 26, 2025)

What we collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.
  • The app has no network features and does not use third-party analytics or SDKs.

Local data on your Mac

  • Settings you change (laser color, size and intensity, hotkeys, line width) are saved locally via macOS UserDefaults.
  • Drawings are not saved. They exist only in memory while the app runs.

Permissions

  • The app does not request Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission.
  • It reads the cursor position and the state of the modifier keys you configure, and uses registered keyboard shortcuts. Keystrokes are not recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Children's privacy

The app does not target children and has no online services or accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the app's release notes with the effective date.

  • 커서 위치와 사용자가 지정한 수정 키(⌘·⌥ 등)의 눌림 상태를 읽고, 등록한 단축키를 사용합니다. 키 입력은 기록·저장·전송하지 않습니다.
